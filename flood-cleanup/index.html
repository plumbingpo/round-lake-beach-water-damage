Floodwater is almost always rated Category 2 (gray water) or Category 3 (black water), so it carries bacteria, contaminants and possibly hazardous material that can make people seriously ill. Warning Signs for Flood Cleanup

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Heavy rainfall, a dead sump pump and water spreading across the basement
  • Roof or wall openings left by a storm, with rain pouring through
  • Mud, silt or debris left on floors once the water goes down
  • Tide marks on drywall or trim where the water peaked
  • Water pooled on any floor of the building, no matter where it came from
  • An overflowing washing machine, dishwasher or water heater
  • Ground so saturated that puddles sit against the foundation walls

Maintenance Tips for Flood Cleanup

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Add downspout extensions so roof water empties well away from the foundation
  • Check that grading on all sides of the house carries water away from the foundation
  • A backwater valve on your sewer line keeps a backup from pushing sewage indoors
  • A battery-powered backup sump pump keeps water moving if the electricity fails in a storm
  • Keep gutters and storm drains clear of debris so water can flow properly
  • Coat basement walls with waterproofing and seal any cracks in the foundation

Why Hire a Professional for Flood Cleanup?

Getting the standing water out is only one part of cleaning up after a flood. Floodwater carries bacteria, sewage, chemicals and other contaminants that are serious health hazards. A trained cleanup team knows how to set containment, wear PPE (personal protective equipment) correctly and sanitize by protocol, which protects your household from hepatitis, E. coli and other diseases carried in water.

Beyond the health concerns, a poor flood cleanup often leaves hidden moisture that turns into severe mold growth within days. Professional technicians use moisture meters, thermal imaging and psychrometric calculations to make sure every affected area, including wall cavities, subfloors and framing, reaches an acceptable drying standard. Drying by measurement is the one dependable method for avoiding secondary damage, which can add thousands to the repair bill.
