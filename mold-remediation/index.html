Maintenance Tips for Mold Remediation

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Give the crawl space proper ventilation, and think about adding a vapor barrier over the soil
  • Repair any leak inside 24 hours, since 24 to 48 hours of moisture is all mold requires to begin
  • Clear gutters and downspouts regularly, which keeps rain from soaking the foundation walls
  • Keep indoor humidity below 60% (ideally 30 to 50%) with dehumidifiers and proper ventilation
  • Mold in ductwork is prevented by servicing the heating and cooling system and replacing its filters on schedule
  • Deal quickly with condensation that forms on pipes, cold surfaces or window glass

Signs It Is Time for Mold Remediation

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Warping, blistered paint or wallpaper lifting off the wall, all clues to moisture you cannot see
  • Discolored or darkened spots near HVAC vents, across ceilings or on walls
  • Fuzzy or spotted growth on stored boxes, clothing or the backs of furniture
  • Condensation forming regularly on windows, walls or cold surfaces
  • Closets, bathrooms or other damp, enclosed rooms that always smell musty
  • A flood or leak in the recent past that never got a professional dry-out
  • Patches of mold, whatever the color, on a ceiling, wall, floor or other surface
