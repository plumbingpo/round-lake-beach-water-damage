Steps to Take When a Pipe Bursts in Round Lake Beach, IL

Home / Blog / What to Do When a Pipe Bursts in Round Lake Beach, IL

For most homeowners, a pipe bursting ranks among the worst plumbing emergencies there are. Water pours out of a burst line at a rate of 4 to 8 gallons a minute, so within hours rooms may be flooded, possessions damaged, and building materials weakened. Act correctly in those opening minutes and you may spare yourself thousands of dollars in losses, turning a potential major restoration job into a repair you can manage. Below, Round Lake Beach Restoration lays out a step by step plan that homeowners in Round Lake Beach, IL can follow when it happens.

Step 1: Shut Off the Water Supply Immediately

Your first job after a pipe breaks is simple: get the water shut off as soon as you possibly can. Damage and repair bills grow with each minute the leak runs. Here's what to do: locate your main water shutoff valve. Homes in Round Lake Beach usually have it close to the meter: check the basement, the garage, a utility room, or an outside wall facing the street. Rotating it clockwise, the old righty-tighty rule, cuts water to the whole home. When the broken pipe is easy to spot and has a dedicated valve close by, shut that one too.

Your water company can cut the supply at the meter, so call them at once if the valve is missing or will not budge. It is also why every homeowner should learn where the shutoff is long before anything goes wrong. Take a few minutes today to locate yours and make sure it operates smoothly. If it's stuck, have a plumber service it before you need it in an emergency.

Step 3: Limit the Damage to Your Things

There is useful work to do until help shows up: first, carry anything valuable, plus paperwork and electronics, to a dry room. Slip plastic blocks or aluminum foil beneath furniture legs so nothing sits directly on soaked carpet. Pick up any area rugs lying on wet flooring. Mop up accessible standing water with towels. Open closet and cabinet doors to promote air circulation. If you can reach the breaker panel safely and stay out of the water, switch off power to the affected rooms.

Never walk through standing water near electrical outlets, appliances, or panels. When flooding is heavy and there is no safe route to the panel, do not try to shut the power off; let professionals evaluate it first. Safety always comes first.

Step 4: Document Everything for Insurance

Hold off on cleanup until you have a thorough record of the damage to give your insurer. Take photos and video of all standing water, damaged materials, and affected belongings. Note the date and approximate time you discovered the burst pipe, and document the source of the leak if visible. Round Lake Beach Restoration supplies detailed reports as well, and paired with your notes they strengthen your claim and your case for fair payment.

Lowering Your Burst Pipe Risk in Round Lake Beach

Some bursts cannot be avoided, but Round Lake Beach homeowners lower the odds a great deal when they insulate exposed pipes in unheated spots such as attics, crawl spaces, and garages. During cold weather, keep faucets dripping slightly to maintain water movement. Warm room air should be able to reach pipes on exterior walls, so open the cabinets under those sinks. Monitor your water pressure and install a pressure reducing valve if pressure exceeds 80 PSI. Replace aging supply lines, especially rubber washing machine hoses, with braided stainless steel. Finally, learn where the shutoff valve is ahead of time.
